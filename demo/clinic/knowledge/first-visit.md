# Your first visit

## What should I bring to my first visit?
A photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, and a list of any medicines you take.

## How long is the first visit?
About 45 minutes: a short health questionnaire, an examination with X-rays and a treatment plan with prices.

## Do you treat nervous patients?
Yes. Tell us when you book. We can schedule a longer appointment, explain every step before we start, and agree on a stop signal.

## Which languages do you speak?
Our team speaks English and Spanish.
