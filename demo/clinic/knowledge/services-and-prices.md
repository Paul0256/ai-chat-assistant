# Services and prices

Prices are for patients without insurance. The final price is confirmed after the dentist examines you.

## Check-up and cleaning
Dental check-up with X-rays: $85. Professional cleaning (scale and polish): $110. Check-up and cleaning together: $175.

## Teeth whitening
In-clinic whitening, one 90-minute visit: $390. Take-home whitening kit with custom trays: $260.

## Fillings
Tooth-coloured composite filling: from $140 per tooth, depending on size.

## Root canal treatment
From $650 for a front tooth and from $950 for a molar, including the follow-up visit.

## Crowns
Ceramic crown: $1,050, usually two visits about two weeks apart.

## Dental implants
Single implant with crown: from $2,900. A free 20-minute implant consultation is available.

## Children's dentistry
Check-up for children under 12: $45. We see children from age 3.

## Emergency appointments
Same-day emergency appointment: $120 for the visit; treatment is priced separately. Call us first so we can fit you in.
