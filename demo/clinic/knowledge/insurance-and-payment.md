# Insurance and payment

## Which insurance do you accept?
We work directly with BrightCare Dental and SmileShield plans. With other insurers you pay at the clinic and we give you an itemised receipt to claim the cost back.

## How can I pay?
Card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), Apple Pay and Google Pay. We do not accept cheques.

## Do you offer payment plans?
Yes. For treatment over $1,000 you can split the cost into 3, 6 or 12 monthly payments with no interest.
