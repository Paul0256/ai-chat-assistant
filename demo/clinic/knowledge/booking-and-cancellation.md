# Booking and cancellations

## How do I book an appointment?
Call us at +1 555 0100 during opening hours, or email hello@riverside-dental.example with your name, phone number and preferred days. We reply within one working day.

## Can I book online?
Online booking is coming soon. For now please call or email us.

## How do I cancel or reschedule?
Please let us know at least 24 hours in advance by phone or email.

## Is there a cancellation fee?
Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice, or missed, are charged a $40 fee. The fee is waived for emergencies and illness.

## How early should I arrive?
Please arrive 10 minutes early for your first visit to fill in a short health form.
