# Opening hours and location

## What are your opening hours?
Monday to Thursday 8:00 to 18:00, Friday 8:00 to 15:00, Saturday 9:00 to 13:00. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Where is the clinic?
48 Riverside Avenue, Springfield, on the ground floor next to the Riverside Pharmacy.

## Is there parking?
Yes. Free parking for patients behind the building; the entrance is from Mill Lane. Bicycle racks are at the front door.

## Can I get there by public transport?
Bus lines 4 and 12 stop at Riverside Avenue, two minutes on foot from the clinic.

## Is the clinic wheelchair accessible?
Yes. The clinic is on the ground floor with step-free access and an accessible restroom.
